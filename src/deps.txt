# The groups each src/ group may include directly, one line per group in the
# order of src/layers.txt: "group: dep dep ...", deps in layers.txt order (a
# group with no deps is "group:" alone).  A group includes headers from the
# groups listed on its line and from itself, nothing else.
#
# Every dep must sit on a lower line of src/layers.txt.  tools/check_layers.py
# (the check-layers target) fails on an include of a group not listed here, and
# on a listed dep that no include uses, so the list stays exact.  Adding a
# dependency is then a visible one-line change here.
#
# The build gives each group the include paths of its deps and, transitively,
# of their deps: a header may include its own group's deps, so those must
# resolve too.  CMakeLists.txt computes that closure from this file.
